A few practical questions

Let's clear
things up.

Have a question about your school's setup? Send us a note.

Who is Maktab built for?+

Islamic school administrators and the teams who support them, including weekend and supplementary schools, full-time schools and Quran programs. Tell us about your program and we'll talk through how it fits.

Will it be easy for our team to use?+

We designed Maktab around the tasks school admins do every day: reviewing registrations, finding family records and checking payments. Each role gets its own dashboard with the next action on top.

Can parents register siblings together?+

Yes. Parents register every child in the same flow, and your school's sibling discount policy is applied before they submit. Once a family submits, their price is fixed.

Can families pay in installments?+

Yes. Families can pay in full, pay the minimum now, choose a monthly plan of up to 12 installments, or pay any amount toward the balance, by card or from a bank account through Stripe. The choices depend on your school's settings.

What about cash, checks, or Zelle?+

Your staff record payments received outside online checkout against the family's ledger, with an emailed receipt. This is manual recording, not an automatic Zelle or bank integration.

How does re-enrollment work?+

You invite returning families, their seats are held until your reply-by date, and they confirm every child on one page. At year end, the rollover moves each student with a decision you can see and change: promote, retain, graduate or leaving.

How do you protect student records?+

Every read of a student's record in the app is logged and kept for three years. Teachers see only what they need. Your IT admin must give a reason before opening a record, and the people who built Maktab have no login to your school's data. These safeguards help your school meet its own FERPA obligations.

Does it help after registration season?+

Yes. Maktab also brings together student records, classes, schedules, attendance, gradebooks and report cards, with views for administrators, teachers and parents.
