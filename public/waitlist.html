A simple privacy note

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Your color-theme preference is stored in your browser.

When you send the interest form or join the waitlist, your answers are emailed to the Maktab team and kept in a private database so nothing is lost. We use them only to reply to you and to understand what schools need. We don't sell or share them, and we'll delete them if you ask.

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes the request to deliver it. For questions, or to have your details deleted, contact abdullah.siddiki@darulislah.org.
